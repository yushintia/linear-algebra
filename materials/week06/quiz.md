# Week 6 Self-Check Quiz

Linear Algebra (501976-001) · Not graded. Answer alone, no notes.
About 10 minutes.

---

**1.** What is the determinant of `[[2, 3], [1, 4]]`?

A. `5`
B. `11`
C. `-5`
D. `14`

**2.** A system's determinant is nonzero. What does this tell you?

A. The system has no solution.
B. The system has exactly one solution.
C. The system has infinitely many solutions.
D. Nothing; the determinant does not connect to solutions.

**3.** A system's determinant is zero. What does this tell you?

A. The system has exactly one solution.
B. The system is definitely inconsistent.
C. The system does not have exactly one solution; it has none or infinitely many.
D. Nothing at all.

**4.** In cofactor expansion, what decides each term's sign?

A. A random choice made while computing.
B. An alternating plus/minus checkerboard pattern.
C. The sign is always positive.
D. The size of the matrix entry.

**5.** Which best defines a "minor"?

A. A synonym for determinant.
B. The smaller grid left after removing one row and one column.
C. Any 2x2 matrix, regardless of where it came from.
D. The largest entry in a matrix.

**6.** Two rows of a matrix are identical. What is true about its
determinant?

A. It is always `1`.
B. It is always negative.
C. It is exactly `0`.
D. It cannot be computed.

**7. (Short answer)** Compute the determinant of `[[1, 1], [2, 5]]`.
Does the corresponding system have exactly one solution? Show your
work.
